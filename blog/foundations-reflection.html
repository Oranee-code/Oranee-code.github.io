Human Skills Reflection


What are three new things you have learnt about yourself and your ego due to the Human Skills learning?

  • I push myself too hard. Even when I know I need a break, I keep going. I feel like I don’t deserve rest unless I’ve done everything perfectly,
    but I’m learning that rest is part of the process.
  • I leave asking for help until the last minute. I don’t want to bother others or seem like I don’t know what I’m doing, so I wait.
    But I’m learning it’s okay to ask for help early and thatit doesn’t mean I’ve failed.
  • I get anxious easily. I start to sweat and feel nervous when something doesn’t go the way I expect. Even small things can make me feel overwhelmed,
    and my mind starts racing with worry.

What are the role of values, empathy, and self-awareness in learning and programming?

  • Values help us work better with others by encouraging honesty, teamwork, and fairness.
  • Empathy improves communication and helps us create solutions that meet real needs.
  • Self-awareness lets us recognize our habits and grow by understanding ourselves better.

What has surprised you the most about the Human Skills learning?

I didn’t realize how much my mindset impacts how I learn. I used to think programming was just about logic, but things like communication,
staying calm under pressure, and giving feedback really help me grow as a developer.

What were the most challenging aspects of the Human Skills learning?

The most challenging part of Human Skills learning was being honest with myself when I needed rest.
I often push myself too hard, feel nervous speaking in front of others, and struggle to slow down or reflect without feeling guilty.

Why do you think we, a programming school, are spending so much time focusing on Human Skills learning in a web development Bootcamp course?

I think it’s important to train your mind as well as your coding skills. If you can understand and manage your emotions, it helps you stay calm, focused,
and work better with others. Human Skills are just as important as technical skills because they help you grow as a person and a developer.

Does the time you spent studying Human Skills learning here feel like a waste of time? Should you have just used that time to practise programming instead? Justify your answer.

No, I don’t think it was a waste of time. I didn’t realise how important Human Skills were until we actually did them.
They helped me understand myself better and made me feel more prepared to handle challenges, not just in coding but in life too.
It doesn’t matter how high your IQ is; if you have low emotional intelligence (EQ), you’ll struggle to manage your emotions and work well with others.
